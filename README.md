# 🛫 Airline Nexus - Airline Management Simulator

**Airline Nexus** is an immersive airline management simulation game where you build, grow, and manage your own airline empire from the ground up. Take on the role of an airline CEO and make strategic decisions that will determine your success in the competitive aviation industry.

## 🎮 Game Features

- **Fleet Management**: Choose from 50+ aircraft types, from regional jets to jumbo jets
- **Route Planning**: Connect airports worldwide and build your global route network
- **Financial Strategy**: Manage budgets, take loans, and grow your airline empire
- **Staff Management**: Hire and train pilots, crew, and ground staff
- **Realistic Aviation**: Experience authentic airline operations with fuel costs, maintenance, and scheduling
- **Global Competition**: Navigate market dynamics and compete with other airlines
- **Business Models**: Choose between different business models (full-service, low-cost, etc.)

## 🌍 What You'll Experience

Build your airline from a small startup to a global carrier. Make strategic decisions about:
- Aircraft acquisition and fleet expansion
- Route network optimization
- Staff recruitment and training
- Financial management and profitability
- Market positioning and competition

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Create Your Airline**: Start with a small operation and name your carrier
2. **Build Your Fleet**: Purchase aircraft and assign them to routes
3. **Expand Globally**: Connect airports and grow your network
4. **Manage Finances**: Balance cash flow, take loans, and invest wisely
5. **Achieve Success**: Build a profitable, reputable airline empire

## 🎯 Target Audience

- Aviation enthusiasts and simulation game fans
- Strategy and business simulation game lovers
- Anyone interested in airline operations and management
- Players who enjoy long-term strategic planning games

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- React 19 with TypeScript
- Three.js for 3D visualizations
- Zustand for state management
- Tailwind CSS for styling
- Framer Motion for animations
- Vite for build tooling

## 📦 Requirements

- Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- JavaScript/TypeScript knowledge (for modding/customization)

## 🎯 Game Objective

The goal is to build a successful, profitable airline that can compete globally. Balance growth with financial responsibility, optimize your route network, and establish your airline as a major player in the aviation industry.
